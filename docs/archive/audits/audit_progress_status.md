## 🤖 LLM Metadata Standard (Template)

### Applied to Consolidation Documents:

```yaml
---
document_metadata:
  title: "Document Title"
  created: "2025-10-06T19:00:00Z"
  last_updated: "2025-10-06T19:00:00Z"
  version: "1.0.0"
  status: "active" | "archived" | "deprecated"
  document_type: "guide" | "report" | "architecture" | "reference"
  consolidates: [number of source documents]
  
tags:
  primary: ["#category1", "#category2"]
  secondary: ["#tag1", "#tag2"]
  temporal: ["#year-quarter"]
  
related_documents:
  parent: ["../path/to/parent.md"]
  successor: ["./next-doc.md"]
  predecessor: ["./prev-doc.md"]
  related: ["../related/doc.md"]
  source_files: ["./original1.md", "./original2.md"]
  
semantic_context:
  summary: "One-sentence description"
  key_topics: ["topic1", "topic2"]
  entities: ["entity1", "entity2"]
  milestones: ["milestone1", "milestone2"]

---
```
