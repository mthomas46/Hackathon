1. **Duplicate Detection**: The worker checks if the document is a duplicate by comparing its content hash with existing documents in the database.
2. **Metadata Enrichment**: If the document is a duplicate, the worker enriches its metadata by merging arrays (tags, categories), updating scalars if missing, and preserving original timestamps.

If the document is not a duplicate, the worker proceeds to:

1. **Processing**: The worker processes the document as usual, generating embeddings if necessary.
2. **Error Handling**: If an error occurs during processing, the worker retries the operation with exponential backoff (1s, 2s, 4s) up to three attempts.

The final outcome of the processing is:

* **Success**: The document was processed successfully and its metadata was updated.
* **Skipped**: The document was skipped due to being a duplicate, but its metadata was enriched.
* **Failed**: The document failed processing due to an error, and its metadata remains unchanged.
